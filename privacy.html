Privacy Policy

Weapon Ball Z — Data & Privacy Information
Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Overview

Weapon Ball Z ("we", "our", or "the Game") is a mobile and PC game developed and published by Hyntez. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you play the Game, create an account, or use in-app purchases.

By downloading, installing, or playing Weapon Ball Z, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

2. Information We Collect

2.1. Account Information

When you create an account using Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, or Email/Password:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and for support purposes.
  • Display name — your chosen in-game name shown to other players.
  • Profile picture URL — optional, from your Google or Apple account.
  • User ID — a unique identifier assigned by our authentication system (Firebase).
  • Account creation timestamp — when your account was first created.
  • Last login timestamp — when you last signed in.

2.2. Game Progress & Data

We store the following data to sync your progress across devices:

  • In-game currency — gold and diamonds balances.
  • Inventory — weapons, balls, items, and cosmetics you have unlocked or purchased.
  • Game progress — campaign levels cleared, high score, story stages, and daily reward claims.
  • Tournament records — Grand Weekly match history and MMR rating.
  • Purchase history — records of in-app purchases.

2.3. Advertising Data

When you watch rewarded or interstitial advertisements served through Unity LevelPlay (IronSource), the ad network may collect:

  • Device identifier — for ad targeting and frequency capping.
  • Ad view/click records — to track ad completion for reward fulfillment.
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT) status — iOS 14.5+ permission status (if applicable).
Note: We do not have direct access to your device advertising identifier. Ad targeting and personalization are controlled by Unity LevelPlay's platform.

2.4. Basic Device Information

When you play the Game, we may automatically collect:

  • Device type and OS version — for compatibility and bug fixing.
  • Game version — to diagnose version-specific issues.
  • Network timestamp sync — we may sync with trusted time servers (e.g., Google, Microsoft, Cloudflare) to keep the game's clock accurate and consistent across sessions.

2.5. What We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect: passwords (authentication is handled by Google/Apple), precise location data, contacts, SMS, call logs, camera, microphone, storage contents, browsing history, or any data not explicitly listed above.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account across all platforms (Android, iOS, PC).
  • To sync your game progress and inventory to our cloud database so you can continue playing on any device.
  • To power online features: leaderboards, Grand Weekly tournaments, and matchmaking.
  • To process in-app purchases and deliver purchased items.
  • To reward you with in-game currency after watching video advertisements.
  • To prevent and detect cheating, hacking, and time manipulation.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.
  • To push game updates and notify you of new content (via in-game notices).

We do NOT use your personal data for advertising, marketing campaigns, or selling your information to third parties.

5. Data Storage

5.1. Cloud Storage (Firebase Firestore)

Your account information and game progress are stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore, operated by Google. Data is stored on Google's servers and may be processed in data centers located worldwide.

5.2. Local Storage (PlayerPrefs)

Some game data is also stored locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs, including:

  • Display name, last login provider.
  • Game settings (sound, graphics preferences).
  • Temporary weapon/item data cache.
  • Apple Sign-In credentials (on iOS).
  • Pending OAuth deep-link data (for desktop web auth).

This local data is stored only on your device and is not accessible by us. You can clear this data by uninstalling the Game.

6. Data Security

We take the following measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted over HTTPS/TLS.
  • User passwords are never stored or transmitted by us — authentication is fully delegated to Google and Apple.
  • Sensitive server-side operations (IAP verification, currency rewards, tournament logic) run through Firebase Cloud Functions, which prevents client-side tampering.
  • Time synchronization with trusted servers helps keep the game clock consistent and accurate.
  • Only the minimum required data is stored in our cloud database.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information can contact us through the channels in Section 11 to request deletion.

8. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate game data.
  • Right to deletion — request complete deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to revoke access — revoke Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In access at any time from your Google Account permissions page or Apple ID settings.
To request account deletion or data access: Contact us through one of the channels in Section 11. We will respond to verified requests within 30 days. Note that some data (e.g., purchase receipts, tournament records) may be retained for legal or fraud prevention purposes for a longer period.

9. International Data Transfers

As we use Firebase (Google Cloud) and other U.S.-based services, your data may be transferred to and processed in servers located in the United States or other countries. We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Post a notice in-game.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued use of the Game after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
