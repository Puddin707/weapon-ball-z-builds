Privacy Policy

Weapon Ball Z — Data & Privacy Information
Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Weapon Ball Z ("we", "our", or "the Game") collects, uses, and protects information when you sign in with Google and play the Game.

1. Information We Collect

When you choose to sign in with Google, we receive the following information from your Google account, depending on the permissions (scopes) you grant:

  • Email address — used to create and identify your in-game account.
  • Basic profile information — such as your name and profile picture, used to display your identity inside the Game.
  • OpenID identifier — a unique, anonymous user ID used by our authentication system to keep you signed in across devices.

We do not access your Google password, contacts, Gmail, Drive, or any other Google service data. We only request the minimum scopes required to authenticate you: openid, email, and profile.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above only for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your in-game account.
  • To save your game progress, settings, and unlocked content to our cloud database.
  • To allow sign-in across multiple devices using the same Google account.
  • To provide online features such as leaderboards, friend lists, or matchmaking.

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the Game. These services may process your data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — handles Google Sign-In and account management. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase Realtime Database / Firestore — stores your in-game progress and settings. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0) — used to verify your identity securely. (Google Privacy Policy)

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

  • All authentication traffic is encrypted over HTTPS / TLS.
  • Passwords are never stored or transmitted by us — authentication is fully delegated to Google.
  • Only the minimum required data is stored in our backend database.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, please contact us so we can delete the associated account.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Revoke Google Sign-In access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
To request account deletion, please contact us through one of the channels listed in Section 8. We will delete your account within 30 days of your request.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

The Game itself does not use cookies or browser tracking. Our website (puddin707.github.io/weapon-ball-z-builds) may use minimal local storage to remember your preferences (such as dark/light mode), but does not set tracking cookies.

By using Weapon Ball Z and signing in with Google, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.
